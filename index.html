RevOps Bulletin & Field Guidelines

Deal hygiene & operational rules for Trimble 7–12 day SMB cycles

Field Rules
7–12 Day Velocity Window

With an agile 7–12 day sales cycle (~1.5 to 2 weeks), outbound launched even in Weeks 10–11 can comfortably book this quarter. Never halt prospecting early.

⚡ Fast Runway Advantage
20-Day Stale Deal Limit

Any SMB opportunity stalled in Stage 2 (Needs) or Stage 3 (Proposal) for >20 days is twice the median cycle. Win rate drops under 10%—requalify or close-lost immediately.

⚠️ Pipeline Hygiene Alert
24-Hour Quote Turnaround

In rapid SMB motions, delivering a formal quote or contract within 24 hours of discovery increases win rate by over 40%. Momentum is the primary driver of 7–12 day closes.

📈 Speed-to-Contract
Protect Against Post-Quota Valley

Stopping outbound when pacing ahead creates an instant pipeline vacuum in Month 1 of next quarter. Always maintain steady outreach each week.
